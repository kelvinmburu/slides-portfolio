How a project runs

The same five stages, whether it's a single slide fix or a full deck build.

01

Brief & source review

Share the audience, the ask, and whatever source material exists — analysis, notes, or an old deck.

02

Storyline & structure

A slide-by-slide outline and argument flow, agreed before any design work starts.

03

Slide build

Full drafting in PowerPoint — layouts, charts, and iconography built natively, not pasted images.

04

Polish & QA

Alignment, consistency, and a line-by-line check against the source numbers.

05

Delivery

Final .pptx, editable and on-brand, with source files if requested.
